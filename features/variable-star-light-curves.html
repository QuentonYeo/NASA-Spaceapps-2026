Variable-star light curves

Open RW Aurigae or R Draconis from Validate a known object. Once the frames finish loading, open the Static analysis tab and select Measure brightness under Brightness over time near the title, above the frame timeline. Measuring and switching wavelength groups open a measured exposure if the current frame is outside the selected group. Select Show measured pixels on frame to jump to the image, or choose a specific exposure under Frame measurements. A cyan overlay shows every pixel contributing to the surrounding annulus. Partial pixels use lighter shading, and the core stays clear. In Aligned Blink, the overlay projects the original native pixel footprints onto the aligned preview. The background annulus is used for subtraction and is excluded from the overlay. The panel lists skipped exposures and the reason.

The backend uses retained native FITS data and the preset’s sky coordinates. It projects the fixed position to each exposure and measures a fractional-pixel annulus around the core. Core pixels are excluded because the cached SPHEREx exposures frequently flag them for overflow, saturation-related artifacts, or other quality issues. The target annulus spans 2.5–4.5 native pixels for RW Aurigae and 6.5–8.5 for R Draconis. The respective background annuli span 6–9 and 10–13 pixels. A sigma-clipped local mean is subtracted before converting the weighted sum from MJy/sr to mJy using the exposure’s WCS pixel scale.

The measurement requires a full target annulus with valid, unflagged pixels. It skips exposures without adequate background samples, wavelength metadata, or usable FITS data. The graph groups points by detector and local wavelength in bins of about 2%, defaulting to a group with the most observing dates. Use Compare at above the chart to select a wavelength group. Each option shows its wavelength or range, detector, and number of observing dates. Groups with more dates appear first. A group with one date can show the measured brightness for that date.

Wing flux is a relative brightness proxy. It excludes the core and has no aperture correction. Numerical 1-sigma statistical uncertainty is supplied in point tooltips when a matching FITS VARIANCE plane has finite positive values for every contributing wing pixel and retained background sample. Unknown uncertainty is returned as null; the flux remains available. Error bars are not drawn. For independent pixels, the propagated variance is sum(w^2 V) + sum(w)^2 sum(Vbg)/Nbg^2, converted to mJy with the same WCS solid angle as flux. The background term uses only samples retained by sigma clipping. This estimate is conditional on that clipping and omits pixel covariance, calibration, background structure and PSF systematics. Bounds include measured flux with padding; selected points retain their contrasting markers and tooltips report uncertainty. The comparison does not correct for changes in point spread function, detector response, or local wavelength within a group. The graph supports visual inspection of the measurements and does not confirm variability or establish its significance.
